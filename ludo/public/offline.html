For four players

Ludo

Bring your four tokens around the board and home before anyone else does.

How to play
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token onto the board. Tokens travel clockwise.
  • A 6 gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row lose your turn.
  • Land on a rival token to send it back to its yard, unless it stands on a star or a start square.
  • Capturing a token or getting one home gives you another roll.
  • You need the exact number to reach the centre. The first player to bring all four tokens home wins, and the rest play on for the other places.
