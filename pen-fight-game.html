About Pen Fight

Almost everyone who went to school in India remembers a bored moment at the back of a classroom with a friend, two pens, and a desk. Pen Fight turns that old classroom duel into a quick game you can play anywhere. No teacher will confiscate it.

Each round is a short duel, so it is easy to play one game in a break, or keep going just to win a rematch against a friend sitting next to you.

How to play

  1. Press Play now to open the game on this page.
  2. Follow the on-screen instructions at the start of the duel. They show how to aim and flick your pen.
  3. Win the duel to beat your opponent.
  4. Play again to try for a better result.

Tips for winning duels

  • Take your time. A careful, measured shot usually beats a fast, wild one.
  • Learn how the pen moves. Play a few practice rounds to get a feel for how much force sends the pen where.
  • Watch your opponent. Pay attention to what they do each round and adjust your next move.
  • Stay calm after a loss. One bad round doesn't decide the game.

A classroom classic

Pen games have been part of school life for generations, and every school has its own version with its own rules, names, and house arguments about what counts. That is a big part of the fun. This game keeps the heart of it: two players, one pen each, and a duel to settle who is better.
