Mudline

Five soldiers, one shell-torn field. Respawn as often as you like. First to the kill target holds the line.

Touch controls: left thumb moves (push to the edge to sprint), right thumb drags to aim. Buttons on the right fire, jump and reload. Use your phone in landscape.
