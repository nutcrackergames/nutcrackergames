Mudline: everything you need to know

Game guide · Updated November 2026

Genre
Multiplayer PvP war game
Players
4-player matches, everyone against everyone
Goal
Be first to reach the kill target
Platform
Your web browser, free, no download

Mudline drops you onto a ruined battlefield of trenches, rubble, and burned-out buildings, with smoke hanging over the skyline. It is a free-for-all: there are no teams, so every other player is a target and every other player is a threat.

The rule that decides each match is simple. Every elimination counts toward the kill target, and the first player to reach it wins. That makes Mudline a game of judgement as much as aim. A player who picks safe fights and stays alive can finish ahead of a faster player who keeps getting eliminated.

Why you might like it

Matches are tense and quick to understand. You can jump in, learn the basics in one round, and still find new ways to play well after many more. It suits players who enjoy action and a bit of bluffing, because in a four-way fight the best move is often to let two other players weaken each other first.

Quick tips

  • Fight from cover and keep an exit route.
  • Be careful when you are busy with one player, because a third player may be watching.
  • Check the score often. The player nearest the target is the one to stop.

Only Down: everything you need to know

Game guide · Updated November 2026

Genre
Platformer
Goal
Reach the bottom by jumping from platform to platform
Best for
Patient players who like a challenge
Platform
Your web browser, free, no download

Only Down flips the classic platformer. Instead of climbing toward the sky, you are trying to get to the bottom, dropping from floating platform to floating platform without missing a landing. The hero is a kid in a red hoodie leaping across grassy blocks that hang in the air.

Going down sounds easy until you try it. Falling is fast, so you have less time to judge where you will land, and a platform that looked close can pass by in a moment.

Why you might like it

It is the kind of game where one more try always feels possible. Each attempt teaches you more of the route, and the satisfaction comes from finally making a section that stopped you before.

Quick tips

  • Look at the next platform before you leave the current one.
  • Short hops are easier to control than long leaps.
  • If a section keeps beating you, take a short break and come back calm.

Pen Fight: everything you need to know

Game guide · Updated November 2026

Genre
Classic classroom duel
Goal
Win the duel against your opponent
Best for
Quick rounds and rematches with friends
Platform
Your web browser, free, no download

Pen Fight is built on a memory that many people who went to school in India share: a boring period, two pens, a desk, and a friend on the other side. The game takes that classroom duel and puts it in your browser, so you can play it any time without a teacher noticing.

Every round is a short duel. That makes it ideal for a quick break, and it makes rematches very easy to ask for.

Why you might like it

It is nostalgic, instantly understandable, and quick. Even if you never played the real thing, the idea of a head-to-head duel is easy to enjoy.

Quick tips

  • Take a few practice rounds to learn how your move controls the pen.
  • A careful shot usually beats a wild one.
  • Notice what your opponent does and change your plan.

Ludo: everything you need to know

Game guide · Updated November 2026

Genre
Classic dice board game
Players
Two to four in the classic game
Goal
Bring all four of your tokens home first
Platform
Your web browser, free, no download

Ludo is one of the most familiar family board games in the world. It grew out of Pachisi, a race game played in India for centuries, and the version known as Ludo was patented in England in 1896. The basic idea has stayed the same: roll the die, move your tokens around the board, and try to get all four home before anyone else.

It is simple enough to learn in a minute, and the mix of luck and choice keeps it fun for both children and adults.

Why you might like it

Ludo is relaxed and social. A game can swing in the last few turns, so nobody is ever completely out of it, which makes it a good pick when you want something easy and friendly.

Quick tips

  • Bring tokens out early so you have more choices each turn.
  • Use the star squares, which are safe from capture.
  • Remember that a token with an opponent up to six squares behind it can be hit by a single roll.

Space Simulator: everything you need to know

Game guide · Updated November 2026

Genre
Space simulator
Theme
Planets, moons, and orbits
Best for
Curious players who like to explore
Platform
Your web browser, free, no download

Space Simulator is a calm, curious game about space. Its world is made of planets, moons, and the orbits that hold them together, and it is made for anyone who has looked at the night sky and wondered how it all fits together.

It also pairs well with a few real facts. Earth is about 150 million kilometres from the Sun, and its light takes just over eight minutes to reach us. The Moon is about 384,400 kilometres away, and light crosses that gap in just over a second.

Why you might like it

It is a relaxed game with no hurry. If you enjoy learning how things work, space is a great place to explore, and the game gives you an excuse to think about gravity, speed, and distance.

Quick idea

  • An orbit is a balance between gravity pulling inward and sideways speed. Too slow and you fall inward, too fast and you drift outward.

Plane Simulator: everything you need to know

Game guide · Updated November 2026

Genre
Flight simulator
Goal
Take off, fly, and land an airliner
Best for
Players who enjoy steady, careful control
Platform
Your web browser, free, no download

Plane Simulator puts you in the cockpit of an airliner. You take off from the runway, climb above mountains and coastline, and try to bring the plane back down safely.

A real flight has clear stages: taxi, take-off, climb, cruise, descent and approach, and landing. Thinking in those stages helps you stay calm, because each one only asks for a few things at a time.

Why you might like it

Smooth, patient flying is its own reward. A good landing feels great, and every flight is a chance to get a little smoother. Four forces act on every plane, which are lift, weight, thrust, and drag, and flying well is mostly a matter of keeping them balanced.

Quick tips

  • Make small, gentle control inputs.
  • Line up with the runway early so your final approach is a steady line.
  • Level out gently just before the wheels touch down.

This is a game for fun. Real aircraft are far more complex, and it is not meant for pilot training.
