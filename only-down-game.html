About Only Down

Only Down turns the usual platformer upside down. Instead of climbing toward the top, you are always heading for the bottom. You drop from one floating platform to the next, trying to reach the ground without missing a landing. The hero is a kid in a red hoodie leaping through a world of floating grassy blocks.

Every jump is a small decision: how far to go, where to land, and when to wait. It is easy to understand, and hard to master.

How to play

  1. Press Play now to open the game.
  2. Read the on-screen instructions at the start. They show the controls for your device.
  3. Jump from platform to platform, always heading downward.
  4. Land carefully, then plan your next jump before you take it.
  5. Reach the bottom to finish the game. If you miss a landing, take a breath and try again.

Tips for reaching the bottom

  • Look ahead before you jump. Check where the next platform is before you leave the one you are on.
  • Use small hops. Short, controlled jumps are easier to land than big leaps.
  • Do not rush. Falling feels fast, so a calm player usually gets further than a hasty one.
  • Learn the layout. Each attempt shows you more of the route, so a second try is often much better than the first.
  • Take breaks. If a tricky section keeps beating you, step away for a minute and come back fresh.

Why going down is tricky

Moving down is harder to judge than moving up. When you fall, you cover distance quickly, so you have less time to work out where you will land. A platform that looked close can pass by in a moment. That is why patience, and looking a step or two ahead, matter more here than fast reactions.
