About Mudline

Mudline is a free multiplayer war game that runs in your browser. The setting is a broken battlefield: trenches, piles of rubble, burned-out buildings, and smoke over the skyline. There are no teams to hide behind. Every player fights every other player, so each match is a scramble of quick decisions about when to attack and when to back off.

The goal is simple. Each time you eliminate another player, it counts toward the match's kill target, and the first player to reach that target wins. Because the winner is whoever gets there first, a calm and careful player can beat a reckless one who keeps getting eliminated.

How to play

  1. Press Play now on this page. The game opens right here on Nutcracker Games, with nothing to download.
  2. Read the on-screen instructions when the match starts. They show the controls for your device.
  3. Find other players and eliminate them. Every elimination adds to your score.
  4. Keep an eye on the scoreboard. The first player to reach the kill target wins.

Tips for winning

  • Use cover. Ruins and trenches are there for a reason. Fighting from cover gives you time to react.
  • Beware of third parties. In a match where everyone is an enemy, a player who is busy fighting someone else is easy to catch. But the same is true for you, so look around before you commit to a fight.
  • Don't chase every kill. A long chase can drag you into open ground. Pick fights you are already winning.
  • Watch the score. If someone is close to the target, they become the main threat, and stopping them matters more than adding to your own count.
