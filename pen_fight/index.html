HOW TO PLAY

  • Apni pen ko pakdo, peeche kheencho, aur chhod do. Jitna door kheenchoge, utni power.
  • Opponent ki pen ko desk se neeche gira do. Jo pehle girega, wo haara!
  • Apni pen ko bhi edge se bachao. Zyada power = zyada risk.
  • Dono pen gir gayi? Draw. Dono barbaad!
  • Matches jeet kar coins kamao, naye pens unlock karo.
  • Desktop: mouse drag. Esc = pause, R = restart (pause menu mein).
  • Classroom mein kabhi kabhi Teacher, Earthquake, Eraser ya Exam Bell aa jaata hai. Settings mein band kar sakte ho.
